I am a **Postdoctoral Research Associate at Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL)**, working in the Integrative Microbiomics Group within the Biosciences Division.

My research focuses on **plant–microbe interactions, microbial ecology, mycorrhizal associations, rhizosphere microbiomes, and plant responses to environmental stress**. I use greenhouse experiments, metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, metabolomics, bioinformatics, and ecological approaches to understand how microorganisms influence plant productivity, drought resilience, and soil carbon cycling.

My current research focuses on **Populus–microbiome interactions**, particularly how mycorrhizal fungi and rhizosphere microbial communities influence plant productivity, drought resilience, microbial functional potential, and soil processes relevant to sustainable bioenergy production.

## Research Interests

- Plant–microbe interactions
- Mycorrhizal ecology
- Microbial ecology
- Rhizosphere microbiomes
- Metagenomics and metatranscriptomics
- Multi-omics
- Bioinformatics
- Plant drought resilience
- Soil carbon cycling
- Bioenergy crops
