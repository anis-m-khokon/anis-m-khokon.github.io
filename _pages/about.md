I am a postdoctoral researcher in the **Integrative Microbiomics Group at Oak Ridge National Laboratory**, working at the intersection of microbial ecology, plant–microbe interactions, and bioinformatics.

My research focuses on understanding how **mycorrhizal fungi and rhizosphere microbial communities influence plant productivity, drought resilience, and soil carbon cycling**, particularly in *Populus* and other bioenergy systems.

I combine **greenhouse experiments, metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, metabolomics, and computational approaches** to investigate microbial functions and plant–microbiome interactions under environmental stress.
