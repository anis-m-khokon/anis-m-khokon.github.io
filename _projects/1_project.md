## Overview

This project investigates how mycorrhizal associations influence **Populus productivity, drought resilience, and soil processes**.

I led a greenhouse experiment comparing arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi, ectomycorrhizal fungi, and non-inoculated controls across multiple *Populus* genotypes under contrasting water conditions.

The project integrates plant phenotyping, mycorrhizal assessment, microbial analyses, and soil measurements, with planned **multi-omics analyses including metagenomics, metatranscriptomics, and metabolomics**, to better understand how plant–fungal interactions shape drought responses and belowground processes.
